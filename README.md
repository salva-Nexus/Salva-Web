## What is Salva?

Most people find crypto hard to start with. You need a wallet app, a long secret phrase to write down, special tokens to pay fees, and long strings of letters and numbers just to send money.

Salva removes all of that. You sign up with your email, set a 4-digit PIN, and you have a wallet. No secret phrase to lose. No fee tokens to buy first. Sending money to someone can be as easy as typing their name.

Salva runs on two blockchain networks, **Base** and **BNB Chain**, and the whole thing lives at **[salva-nexus.org](https://salva-nexus.org)**.

---

## Who is it for?

**Everyday users** who want a simple wallet to hold and send money, with no technical knowledge needed.

**Merchants and traders** who buy and sell digital assets for a living and want their own marketplace, with their own prices.

**Anyone who needs naira on-chain.** Salva has its own naira-backed digital currency, so you can hold and move value in a currency you already understand.

---

## What you can do with Salva

### Create a wallet in under a minute
Sign up with your email, verify it with a one-time code, and choose a 4-digit PIN. Your wallet is ready straight away. Your PIN protects every action you take, so no one can move your money without it.

### Send money using names, not addresses
Instead of copying a long wallet address, you can link a simple, memorable name to your wallet, like `yourname@yourbrand`, and let people send to that. You can also own a namespace, which is a brand-style ending for names, and issue names under it.

### Hold NGNS, a digital naira
NGNS is Salva's naira stablecoin, a digital currency designed to stay in step with the Nigerian naira. Your balances can be viewed in naira and in dollars, so you always know what your money is worth.

### Swap in the marketplace
Salva has a peer-to-peer marketplace where you can find who is selling the asset you want, see their price and how much they have available, and swap with a few taps. Every swap shows you the rate and the fee before you confirm, and you can set how much price movement you will accept.

Pairs that Salva has reviewed carry a verified badge. Pairs set up independently by other merchants are clearly labelled, so you know when to be more careful.

### Become a merchant
Anyone can run their own market. A merchant can:

- set up their own pool
- choose which asset to sell and which asset to accept in return
- set their own price margin
- add or withdraw stock whenever they like
- pause or remove a market

Prices follow live market data, and merchants decide how much they want to earn on each trade.

### Borrow against what you hold
If you hold supported assets, you can lock them as collateral and borrow NGNS against them without selling what you own. Salva shows you a clear health meter for every position, so you can see how safe it is. You can add collateral, borrow more, repay, or withdraw at any time.

If a position gets close to risky levels, Salva can send you an alert so you have time to act.

### Earn rewards
Salva has a community rewards programme. You earn **SANT points** for activity on the platform, and you can invite friends with your own referral link and earn when they join. Points can be claimed as SANT tokens when claiming is open.

### Stay informed
Turn on notifications and Salva tells you when a transaction goes through, when something needs your attention, and when a loan position needs care. You can switch them off at any time in your settings.

## Simple, predictable costs

Salva shows you the cost before you confirm anything: the fee for a transfer, a swap or a borrow is displayed in naira, up front. You never need to hold a separate token just to pay network fees.

---

## How Salva keeps you safe

- **Your PIN guards everything.** Every transaction needs your 4-digit PIN.
- **Email verification.** New accounts are confirmed with a one-time code sent to your email.
- **Security cool-down.** After you change a sensitive detail like your password or PIN, your account is briefly locked to protect you if someone else was behind the change.
- **Clear warnings.** Before you trade with a market Salva has not reviewed, you are told plainly, and you choose whether to continue.
- **No secret phrase.** Nothing to write down, lose, or have stolen.

---

## Run by the community

Salva is overseen by a group of **validators**. Important protocol decisions, such as changes, upgrades and emergency pauses, are proposed, voted on and only then carried out, with a waiting period before they take effect. No single person can change the rules on their own.
